Need Help?

We are here to help you with any issues or questions about Orbit Blast.

Common Issues

  • App crashes on launch: Make sure you have the latest version installed. Try restarting your device.
  • Ads not loading: Check your internet connection. Ads require an active network connection.
  • Game progress lost: Game progress is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app will remove saved data.
  • Performance issues: Close other apps running in the background and ensure your device has sufficient storage.
