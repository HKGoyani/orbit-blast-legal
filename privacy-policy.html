Last updated: May 27, 2026

Orbit Blast does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App does not require account creation, login, or any form of registration.

The current version of the App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

3. Data Storage

All data generated by the App (game progress, settings, scores) is stored locally on your device. We do not operate any servers that collect or store user data.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from any user, including children under 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations worldwide.

5. Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal information to access, modify, or delete. You can remove all locally stored app data by uninstalling the App from your device.
