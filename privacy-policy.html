1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information directly. However, third-party services integrated into the App may collect certain data:

2. How We Use Information

  • To display advertisements within the App

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to serve ads. On iOS 14+, we request your consent via the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before using your device advertising identifier for personalized ads. You may opt out at any time through your device settings.

4. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our servers. Any data collected by third-party SDKs is governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, please contact us so we can delete it.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You can also reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads through your device settings.
