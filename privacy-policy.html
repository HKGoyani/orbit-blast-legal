Shayona ("we", "our", "us") operates the Orbit Blast mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

Orbit Blast does not directly collect or store personal information. The App does not require account creation, login, or any form of registration.

All game progress, preferences, and settings are stored locally on your device and are not accessible to us.

However, the App integrates third-party services (described in Section 2) that may automatically collect certain data as described in their own privacy policies.

2. Third-Party Services

2a. Google AdMob (Advertising)

Starting with version 1.0.1, the App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (banner, app open, interstitial, and rewarded formats). AdMob may collect the following data to serve and measure ads:

Data TypePurposeLinked to You?
Device Advertising ID (IDFA)Ad targeting and frequency cappingNo
Coarse Location (country / region)Geographically relevant adsNo
App usage and interaction dataAd performance analyticsNo
Crash and performance dataSDK stability diagnosticsNo

The App does not implement an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission prompt. AdMob therefore operates in a privacy-preserving limited-ads mode and does not serve personalised ads based on cross-app tracking unless the user has independently granted tracking permission at the iOS system level (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

You may opt out of interest-based advertising at any time via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or by resetting your Advertising Identifier under the same menu.

Google AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google AdMob Partner Privacy: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

2b. Firebase Analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics (by Google) to understand aggregate, anonymous usage patterns — for example, which screens are visited and how long sessions last. Firebase Analytics may collect:

  • App events and screen views (aggregated, not linked to individuals)
  • Device model and OS version
  • App version and install source

This data is used solely to improve the App. It is not sold or shared for advertising purposes beyond what Google's own policies permit.
Firebase Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

2c. Apple In-App Purchases (StoreKit)

The App offers in-app purchases (Gem Packs and Remove All Ads) processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect, receive, or store your payment or billing information. All purchase transactions are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

3. Remove Ads Purchase

Players who purchase the "Remove All Ads" non-consumable in-app purchase ($3.99) will have all advertisements permanently disabled within the App on the purchasing device. The purchase status is verified via Apple StoreKit and recorded locally on device. No personal data is collected or transmitted by us in connection with this purchase.

To restore a previously purchased Remove All Ads entitlement on a new or reinstalled device, use the "Restore Purchases" flow within the App (or reinstall — the App checks StoreKit receipt automatically on launch).

4. Data Storage

All data generated by the App itself (game progress, settings, high scores, purchase status) is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. We do not operate servers that collect or store user data. Third-party SDKs (AdMob, Firebase) may transmit data to Google's servers as described in their respective privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App does not include user accounts, social features, or direct communication tools.

Third-party advertising via AdMob is configured without personalised targeting. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent regulations worldwide.

6. Your Rights

Since we do not directly collect personal data, there is no personal information held by us to access, modify, or delete. For data held by Google (AdMob / Firebase), you may exercise your rights under applicable law by contacting Google at https://policies.google.com/privacy or by adjusting your device privacy and advertising settings.

You can remove all locally stored App data by uninstalling the App from your device.

We may update this Privacy Policy when we introduce new features or third-party services. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
